## Full description
Paceframe is a mobile-first planning and recovery app designed for people who want to stay productive without running themselves into the ground.

Instead of organizing your day only around deadlines, Paceframe looks at energy, stress, screen fatigue, sleep quality, and routines to help you decide what deserves your best effort right now. It combines adaptive planning, AI coaching, reminders, recovery guidance, and burnout-aware signals in one calm system.

With Paceframe you can:

- choose the next task based on your real energy, not guilt
- track sleep trouble, stress load, screen fatigue, and care basics
- use AI coaching to plan the day, pressure-test choices, and reduce overwhelm
- schedule reminders for food, movement, hydration, and rest
- protect your workload with recovery-first guidance when burnout risk rises

Paceframe is built for founders, creators, operators, and high-agency people who want steadier output, lower cognitive drag, and a healthier pace.
